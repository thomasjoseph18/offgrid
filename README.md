# 💧 Jal Rakshak (जल रक्षक) 

**India's Smart Water Conservation Platform**


## 🚨 The Problem
India is facing one of the worst water crises in its history:
- **600 Million** Indians face extreme water stress.
- **21 Major Cities** (including Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad) are racing towards zero groundwater levels.
- **70%** of our water is contaminated.
- Average daily usage per person is often much higher than the recommended 135 Liters.

## 💡 The Solution
**Jal Rakshak** is a beautifully designed, gamified, and interactive web platform built to raise awareness and drive action toward water conservation at the individual level.

## ✨ Features
- 🌐 **Bilingual Support**: Fully accessible in English and Hindi (EN/HI toggle).
- 📊 **My Impact Dashboard**: Track daily usage, monthly savings, and your 'Green Score'.
- 🚿 **Water Footprint Calculator**: Interactive sliders to calculate your exact daily water consumption and visualize it with a dynamic Chart.js donut chart. Gives personalized tips based on usage.
- 🌧️ **Rainwater Harvesting & ROI Calculator**: Enter your roof area and city to calculate how much water you can harvest annually, money saved, and a 5-Year ROI projection chart.
- 🗺️ **India Water Stress Map**: CSS-grid-based interactive visualization of water stress levels across various Indian states.
- 🏆 **Gamified Challenges**: Accept and complete real-world water-saving challenges to unlock badges and see your progress bar fill up with confetti celebrations!
- 🎨 **Stunning UI/UX**: Dark mode, deep ocean blues, smooth glassmorphism, animated water droplets, wave SVG animations, and responsive design.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Variables, Flexbox/Grid, Animations), Vanilla JavaScript
- **Libraries**: Chart.js (via CDN) for data visualization
- **Storage**: LocalStorage for persisting challenge progress and language preferences
- **Architecture**: Single Page Application (SPA), No Backend required!

## 🚀 How to Run
This is a purely static web application. 
1. Clone the repository or download the files.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
3. No build tools, no `npm install` needed!
